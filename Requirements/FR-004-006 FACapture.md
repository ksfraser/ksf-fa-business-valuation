# FR-004-006 FACapture: FA Capture & Display of Business Valuation Plan

**Related:** BR-004 Business Valuation, FR-004-001..005

## Description
The FrontAccounting module lets an advisor open a client's Business Valuation plan, run the `Ksfraser\BusinessValuation` engines, and display results inline.

## Primary actor
Advisor.

## Main flow
1. Advisor opens Business Valuation Planning from the client menu. 2. System loads plan and runs engines. 3. Results persist against the client record.

## Postconditions
Business Valuation plan results visible and stored for the client.
